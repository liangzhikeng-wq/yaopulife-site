# 首批神话人物插画 Prompt

> 神话原创立意,**绝不直接复制任何影视/动画/游戏/书籍的既有形象**。

## Sun Wukong
```text
Create an original yaopulife myth illustration inspired by Sun Wukong.

Subject:
A stone-born monkey hero standing on a simple mountain rock, looking toward the sky.

Emotion:
Rebellion, courage, destiny.

Cultural symbols:
Mountain stone, cloud pattern, cinnabar sun.

Composition:
Editorial story cover. The character is small but powerful, surrounded by large negative space.

Style:
New Chinese healing narrative illustration. Ink-inspired linework, warm paper texture, cinnabar accent, muted jade cloud. Mythic but not aggressive.

Avoid:
Do not copy any existing Sun Wukong design from film, anime, games or books. No golden armor overload. No game poster style.
```

## Chang'e
```text
Create an original yaopulife myth illustration inspired by Chang'e.

Subject:
A graceful moon woman seen from behind, standing quietly near the full moon.

Emotion:
Longing, loneliness, beauty.

Cultural symbols:
Full moon, osmanthus branch, cloud ribbon.

Composition:
Moon gate composition. Large moon as quiet emotional center.

Style:
Warm paper background, deep ink linework, pale jade shadow, tiny cinnabar seal. Poetic and restrained.

Avoid:
No glamorous fantasy princess. No crowded palace background.
```

## White Snake
