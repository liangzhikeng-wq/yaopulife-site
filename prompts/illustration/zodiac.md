# 首批 12 生肖插画 Prompt

> 风格统一走 master-style;每条已嵌主体/情绪/符号/构图/避免。

## Rat
```text
Create an original yaopulife zodiac illustration of the Rat.

Subject:
A small clever rat with bright curious eyes, holding a tiny scroll.

Emotion:
Cleverness, alertness, quiet confidence.

Cultural symbols:
Moonlight, rice grains, small red seal stamp.

Composition:
Central character composition. The rat sits slightly below center on warm rice paper, with large negative space above.

Style:
New Chinese healing narrative illustration. Hand-drawn deep ink lines, soft paper texture, cinnabar red accent, muted jade shadow. Cute but not childish, poetic and clean.

Avoid:
No realistic rodent horror. No Disney-like mascot. No crowded background.
```
