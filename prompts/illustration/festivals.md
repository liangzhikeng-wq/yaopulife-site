# 首批传统节日插画 Prompt

## Mid-Autumn Festival
```text
Create an original yaopulife festival illustration for Mid-Autumn Festival.

Subject:
A small family silhouette sharing mooncakes under a full moon.

Emotion:
Reunion, longing, warmth.

Cultural symbols:
Full moon, mooncake, lantern, osmanthus branch.

Composition:
Moon gate composition. Full moon above, small family below, large warm negative space.

Style:
Warm paper texture, deep ink silhouettes, cinnabar lantern accent, muted moon gold glow.

Avoid:
No commercial mooncake advertisement look. No crowded festival poster.
```
