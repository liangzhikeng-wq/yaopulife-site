# Pet Memory Collection Operations Queue

## Direction

Retire the previous test catalog and rebuild yaopulife around pet memory gifts, pet memorial portraits, and custom pet keepsakes.

## Digital Employee Split

- `market-insight-analyst`: monitor keywords, competitor listings, buyer objections, and review language.
- `product-requirement-analyst`: convert each draft into specs, acceptance criteria, and sample requirements.
- `operations-sop-designer`: validate suppliers, packaging, production time, and exception handling.
- `product-image-retoucher`: create image briefs and sample-shot requirements.
- `product-retouch-auditor`: reject misleading mockups or low-trust product imagery.
- `growth-content-strategist`: produce SEO clusters, Pinterest pin briefs, and short-video angles.
- `brand-content-strategist`: keep copy grief-sensitive, warm, and non-exploitative.
- `sales-enablement-coach`: create intake questions, sympathy replies, and follow-up scripts.
- `business-decision-analyst`: rank products by demand, margin, fulfillment risk, and launch readiness.
- `compliance-risk-auditor`: review claims, refund language, logistics promises, and marketplace/IP risk.

## Launch Sequence

1. Validate PET-MEM-001, PET-MEM-003, and PET-MEM-005 first because they are lighter to fulfill.
2. Validate PET-MEM-002 and PET-MEM-007 only after packaging and breakage risk are measured.
3. Prepare PET-MEM-008 before the Q4 holiday window.
4. Keep PET-MEM-010 under compliance review because grief-related copy and delivery promises carry higher risk.

## Automation Boundary

Agent OS may automatically research, draft, score, brief images, create content packs, and prepare work orders. It must not automatically publish checkout products, change prices, change refund/logistics policy, or send customer promises without an approval record.
